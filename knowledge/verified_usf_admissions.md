# Verified USF Graduate Admissions Information

Last reviewed: November 18, 2026

## Applying to a USF graduate program

Applicants submit the USF online graduate application and pay the nonrefundable application fee shown by Graduate Admissions. Applicants must arrange official transcripts from every higher-education institution they attended. Unofficial transcripts uploaded to the application portal may speed up the initial review, but admission is not finalized until USF receives the required official transcripts.

If a program requires test scores, applicants must have the testing agency send official scores directly to USF. Applicants also upload any program-specific materials, which may include a resume, statement of purpose, recommendations, portfolio, writing sample, or other documents. Requirements vary by graduate program, so applicants should check the current program page.

All graduate applicants must answer the conduct-clearance questions in the application. Questions about the general graduate application can be sent to GradAdmissions@usf.edu.

Official source: https://www.usf.edu/admissions/graduate/admission-information/how-to-apply.aspx

## General graduate academic requirements

USF's general minimum graduate requirement includes a bachelor's degree with a 3.00 average on a 4.00 scale in the applicable undergraduate or graduate work described by Graduate Admissions. Individual programs may require additional materials, higher qualifications, test scores, recommendations, a statement of purpose, or a resume. Applicants from countries where English is not the official language must meet USF's current English-proficiency requirements.

Applicants should use the official academic-requirements page for the precise rule that applies to their education history and should confirm additional requirements with their program.

Official source: https://www.usf.edu/admissions/graduate/admission-information/academic-requirements.aspx

## Checking an application and next steps

After applying, students should confirm that the application fee and all program-specific materials have been received. The required program materials may include a statement, resume or CV, transcripts, recommendations, test scores, a portfolio, or a writing sample. Applicants can check their status through USF's graduate applicant resources. USF sends admission notifications by email and/or postal mail, so applicants should monitor their email.

Official sources:

- https://www.usf.edu/admissions/graduate/next-steps/check-application-status.aspx
- https://www.usf.edu/admissions/graduate/next-steps/

## International students and immigration questions

International applicants seeking a graduate degree first apply through USF Graduate Admissions. After a student is admitted, USF International Services assists with the immigration documents needed for a student visa. Students seeking F-1 status need an I-20 from International Services, and students seeking J-1 status need a DS-2019 from USF or another authorized sponsor.

Online programs are not eligible for an I-20. Visa rules and individual situations require authoritative advice, so students should use USF International Services for current guidance rather than relying on the chatbot.

Official sources:

- https://www.usf.edu/world/international-services/before-you-arrive/index.aspx
- https://www.usf.edu/world/international-services/immigration/
